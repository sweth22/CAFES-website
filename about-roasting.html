CAFES • ROASTING

FIRE, TIMING,
AND TINY DETAILS.

Every batch is roasted in small drums to bring out sweetness, balance, and the character of each origin.

Small-batch Profiled roasts Fresh every week

STAGES OF THE ROAST

From pale green to deep mahogany.

1 • DRYING

Green to Straw

Moisture escapes and the beans move from pale green to light straw in color.

2 • MAILLARD

Sweetness Builds

Complex browning reactions develop sweetness, body, and bread‑like aromas.

3 • FIRST CRACK

Beans Expand

The beans audibly crack as they expand, marking the start of drinkable roasts.

4 • DEVELOPMENT

Dialed for CAFES

Heat is adjusted to balance chocolate, fruit, and caramel notes for our menu.

OUR ROASTING PROFILES

Light Roast

Citrus, florals, higher acidity; used for pour‑overs and filter brews.

Best for: Filter bar

Medium Roast

Balanced sweetness and body; our everyday choice for milk coffees.

Best for: Lattes & cappuccinos

Dark Roast

Lower acidity, bold chocolate and smoke notes for intense espresso shots.

Best for: Espresso tonic & mochas

ROASTED IN SMALL DRUMS, NEVER RUSHED.

We log every batch, track temperature curves, and cup daily to keep each bag consistent.
