Open roles

Pick a role to see what your day could look like.

Barista

You live for the sound of steaming milk and the smell of fresh espresso.

  • Craft espresso drinks and recommend desserts.
  • Keep the bar spotless and flowing smoothly.
  • Make regulars feel like they’re at home.
Full‑time · Part‑time · Evenings

Pastry Chef

Early mornings, warm ovens, and recipes that bring people back.

  • Prepare cakes, tarts, cookies, and seasonal specials.
  • Collaborate with baristas on perfect pairings.
  • Help style the dessert display each day.
Full‑time · Morning shifts

Guest Host

You’re the first smile guests see when they walk through the door.

  • Welcome guests, guide them to seats, and manage light queues.
  • Support takeaway and online order pickups.
  • Help keep the space calm, cozy, and inviting.
Part‑time · Weekends

Café Manager

Lead the crew, own the vibe, and keep operations running smoothly.

  • Coach and schedule the team for smooth, fun shifts.
  • Oversee stock, suppliers, and daily quality checks.
  • Bring ideas for events, partnerships, and improvements.
Full‑time · Leadership

Apply in a minute

Drop a few details and we’ll get back to you as soon as a shift opens.

You’ll receive an email confirmation as soon as we receive your application.

What happens next?

  1. Our team reviews your application within a few days.
  2. If there’s a match, we’ll invite you for a coffee chat at the café.
  3. You’ll do a short trial shift to feel the pace and the people.

No pressure, no formal suits — just real conversations over good coffee.
