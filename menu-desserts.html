Matcha Pistachio Tiramisu Bars

2025's trending fusion: Earthy matcha, nutty pistachio, and silky tiramisu layers.

Ingredients (Serves 12)

  • 24 ladyfingers
  • 1 cup ceremonial matcha powder
  • 2 cups mascarpone cheese
  • 1 cup shelled pistachios (ground)
  • 1.5 cups heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 cup matcha green tea (cooled)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Quick Steps

  1. Whip cream, mascarpone, sugar, and vanilla to stiff peaks; fold in ground pistachios.
  2. Dip ladyfingers in matcha tea; layer half in 9x13 pan.
  3. Spread half the pistachio cream; repeat layers. Chill 4 hours.
  4. Dust with matcha and top with pistachios. Slice into bars.
